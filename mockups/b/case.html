The challenge

Mandatory cyber-security training is notoriously dry, and employees tune out. The goal was to turn a monotonous compliance topic into a learning experience people would actually engage with.

02

Audience

All employees required to complete annual security training.

03

My approach

I built interactive courseware around point-of-view videos featuring real employees. I wrote the scripts, managed the director, actors and production schedule, and even acted in a scene myself, so the scenarios felt relatable and authentic.
